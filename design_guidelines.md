# Design Guidelines: Unlock SRQ LLC Professional Locksmith Website

## Design Approach: Trust-First Service Provider

**Selected Approach:** Industry Standard with Trust Signals
This is a professional locksmith service website where credibility, immediate accessibility, and clear service communication are paramount. The design must prioritize trustworthiness over aesthetic experimentation.

**Core Principle:** Users in lock emergencies need quick access to services, pricing transparency, and confidence in the provider's legitimacy and expertise.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Deep Professional Blue: 217 91% 25% (trust, security, professionalism)
- Safety Orange/Amber: 38 92% 50% (urgency, 24/7 availability, call-to-action)
- Clean White: Background and content clarity

**Supporting Colors:**
- Charcoal: 220 13% 18% (text, professional contrast)
- Light Gray: 210 17% 95% (section backgrounds, subtle separation)
- Success Green: 142 71% 45% (verification badges, positive confirmations)

**Dark Mode:** Not recommended for emergency service websites - maintain consistent light mode for maximum readability during stress situations.

## Typography

**Font Strategy:**
- Headlines: Bold, authoritative sans-serif (Inter/Roboto weight 700-800)
- Body Text: Clean, highly readable sans-serif (Inter/Roboto weight 400-500)
- Phone Numbers: Extra large, bold weight for immediate visibility
- Emergency CTAs: All caps with letter spacing for urgency

**Hierarchy:**
- H1 (Hero): 3.5rem-4rem, bold
- Service Titles: 2rem-2.5rem, semibold
- Phone/CTA: 1.5rem-2rem, bold
- Body: 1rem-1.125rem, regular line-height 1.6-1.8

## Layout System

**Spacing Units:** Consistent use of 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48 (px equivalent)
- Section padding: py-16 to py-24
- Card spacing: p-6 to p-8
- CTA margins: my-8 to my-12

**Grid Strategy:**
- Service cards: 2-column on tablet, 3-4 column on desktop
- Service area coverage: Multi-column city listings
- Trust signals: Horizontal layout with icons + text

## Component Library

### Hero Section
**Large professional locksmith image** showing technician at work with service vehicle
- Overlay with semi-transparent gradient (blue to transparent)
- Prominent 24/7 emergency phone number (clickable on mobile)
- Service area coverage immediately visible
- Primary CTA: "Call Now" (phone link) + "Get Quote" button

### Service Cards
- Icon representing service type (home, building, car, emergency)
- Service category title
- 2-3 sentence description
- "Learn More" link to dedicated service page
- Hover: Subtle lift effect (shadow increase)

### Trust Signals Section
- Licensed & Insured badge
- 24/7 Availability indicator
- Response time commitment
- Years in business / local ownership
- Customer review rating display
- Accepted payment methods icons

### Service Area Map
Visual representation showing coverage across Sarasota, North Port, Port Charlotte, Englewood, Bradenton
- Interactive or static map with highlighted service zones
- City list with checkmarks

### Contact Forms
- Name, Phone (required), Email, Service Type dropdown
- Message/Details textarea
- Emergency checkbox option
- Clear "Request Service" submission button
- Response time expectation stated below form

### Footer
- Emergency phone number prominently displayed
- Service hours (emphasize 24/7)
- Quick links: Services, Service Areas, About, Contact
- Address and local business information
- Social proof: Review site links
- Payment method icons
- License number display

## Images

### Required Images:
1. **Hero Image:** Professional locksmith technician working on modern lock system OR service van with company branding - full-width, 60-70vh height
2. **Service Category Images:** High-quality photos for residential, commercial, automotive, emergency services
3. **Trust Building:** Photo of local technicians, service vehicle fleet
4. **Service Area:** Map graphic or local landmark imagery

All images should convey professionalism, modern equipment, and local Florida context where appropriate.

## Key UX Principles

**Emergency-First Design:**
- Phone number accessible from every page section
- Click-to-call functionality prominent on mobile
- Service request forms simple and fast to complete
- No barriers to contacting business

**Credibility Markers:**
- Display license/insurance information
- Show real service area specificity (not generic)
- Include response time commitments
- Feature payment options and pricing transparency

**Local Focus:**
- Emphasize Florida service areas throughout
- Use local imagery where relevant
- Mention specific cities/neighborhoods served
- Local phone number format

## Navigation
Sticky header with:
- Company logo (left)
- Emergency phone number (always visible)
- Main nav: Services (dropdown), Service Areas, About, Contact
- "Get Quote" CTA button (right side)

## Animations
Minimal, professional:
- Smooth scroll to sections
- Card hover lift effects
- Form input focus states
- Button press feedback
- NO distracting motion - users may be in stressful emergency situations

This design prioritizes immediate trust, clear service communication, and frictionless contact pathways appropriate for emergency and scheduled locksmith services.
